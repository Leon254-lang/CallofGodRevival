Thank you for your generosity

Together, we make room for hope.

Giving is a personal choice and an expression of gratitude. Every gift, in any amount, helps sustain the shared life of Call of God Revival Ministry International and the care we offer in our community.

We want giving to be simple, thoughtful, and transparent. Please contact the church office for current giving options and information about how contributions are used.
