Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This notice describes how Call of God Revival Ministry International handles information when you visit this website or use its forms and online services. We aim to handle personal data in accordance with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 and applicable regulations. The notice should be read together with our Terms of Use.

Who to contact

The ministry operates this website. For a privacy question or to make a data request, contact us and put “Data protection request” in the subject line:

Information we handle

  • Contact messages: your name, email address, subject, and message.
  • Prayer requests: the message and any name, email address, or phone number you choose to provide. A prayer request may reveal sensitive information, so please share only what is needed and avoid identifying another person without their permission.
  • Live service email alerts: your email address and subscription status and timestamps. We use confirmation and unsubscribe links; confirmation tokens are stored as hashes. An unconfirmed request expires after 24 hours, and a confirmed address is kept until you unsubscribe or request deletion.
  • Church assistant: the question and recent messages you submit (up to eight messages) are sent to OpenAI's API with relevant published church information so it can generate a reply. The website does not save the conversation in its application database. OpenAI processes the request under its own terms and data practices.
  • Live stream chat: a display name you choose and messages you send are visible to people watching and administrators. Chat messages are held in the running server's memory during the broadcast and are cleared when the broadcast ends or the server restarts.
  • Administrator services: administrator email addresses, password hashes, session records, and content or media uploaded to manage the website.
  • Technical information: the website may process your IP address temporarily to limit abuse and protect forms and services. The hosting provider may also create technical logs needed to operate the service.

How we use information

We use information to answer contact and prayer messages, send live alerts you requested, operate and secure the website, administer church content, and provide live streaming and chat. We process information on the basis of your request, consent where requested, and other lawful grounds available under applicable law. You can withdraw consent for an optional subscription by unsubscribing.

Email, hosting, and other service providers

Contact and prayer submissions are emailed to the ministry using the configured mail service; the website application does not save those submissions in its MongoDB database. Copies may remain in the ministry's email account and the mail provider's systems according to their retention practices.

Depending on which features are enabled, information may be processed by Render for website hosting, MongoDB Atlas for the website database and media, OpenAI for assistant replies, LiveKit for live audio/video, the configured SMTP provider for email, and the configured S3-compatible storage provider for broadcast recordings. Some providers may process information outside Kenya. Where this occurs, the ministry will use arrangements required by applicable data-protection law.

Pages may also load YouTube videos and Google Fonts. Following links to services such as Google Maps or Facebook takes you to those providers. Those services have their own privacy notices and may collect information directly from your browser.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described, to meet applicable obligations, or to resolve a matter. Confirmed alert subscriptions remain until you unsubscribe or request deletion; unconfirmed requests expire after 24 hours. Contact and prayer messages are handled in the ministry's email account and are removed when no longer needed, subject to applicable obligations. Administrator records are retained while needed to operate the site. Live chat is temporary as described above. Live recordings, when enabled, are kept in the configured storage according to the ministry's storage and retention settings.

The administrator portal uses a session cookie to keep administrators signed in; it is marked HttpOnly and, in production, Secure and SameSite=Strict. The site uses reasonable technical and organizational measures, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and data-protection rights

Subject to applicable law and any relevant exceptions, you may ask to be informed about, access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data; object to processing; or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To unsubscribe from live alerts, use the link in an alert email. For other requests, contact the ministry using the details above. We may ask for information needed to verify your identity before acting on a request.

You may also make a complaint to Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

Children and changes to this notice

Please do not submit personal information about a child unless you are authorized to do so and it is necessary for your request. We may update this notice when the website or its services change. The effective date above shows when this version was published.
